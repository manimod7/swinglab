Device check

Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser. It checks that WebXR works, measures how often the controllers report a pose, and how steady the pose is when the bat is held still. Do this before anything else; it takes two minutes.

  1. Press Start check and put the headset on.
  2. Hold the bat (or the controller) as still as you can against your chest, then pull the trigger on the other controller. It records for 3 seconds.
  3. Take the headset off. The results appear below. Use Copy results and paste them to me.
