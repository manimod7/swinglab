Every swing, measured.

Put on a Meta Quest 3 with the printed bat, calibrate in under a minute, then face a virtual bowling machine. SwingLab reads the controller pose at tracking rate and scores each swing and shot. No headset? The simulator runs the same drill with a scripted batter.
